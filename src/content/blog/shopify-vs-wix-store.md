# Shopify vs Wix Store: Which One Wins in 2026?

Choosing between Shopify and Wix for your online store comes down to what you actually need. Both platforms sell products, accept payments, and look professional — but they serve very different types of sellers. This guide breaks down every detail so you can pick the right platform before you invest time and money into the wrong one.

## Pricing and Transaction Fees: True Cost Breakdown

### Shopify 2026 Pricing

Shopify has four main plans in 2026. **Basic is $39/mo**, **Shopify is $105/mo**, **Advanced is $399/mo**, and **Plus starts at roughly $2,300/mo** for enterprise sellers. Each step up unlocks lower card processing rates and more staff accounts. For a full breakdown, see our [Shopify pricing plans explained](/shopify-pricing-plans-explained).

Skip Shopify Payments and you pay extra transaction fees on top of your processor's cut — 2% on Basic, 1% on Shopify, 0.5% on Advanced. That stacks fast. For US-based sellers, Shopify Payments is the obvious move.

### Wix 2026 Pricing

Wix runs cheaper on paper. **Business Basic is $17/mo**, **Business Unlimited is $25/mo**, **Business VIP is $35/mo** (Source: Wix, 2026). Wix Payments charges zero platform transaction fees on all plans. Simple math. See our full [Wix eCommerce review](/wix-ecommerce-review) for more detail.

### Real Cost Example: $10K/Month Store

Say your store does $10,000 per month. On **Shopify Basic with Shopify Payments**, you pay $39/mo plus 2.9% + $0.30 per transaction. At an average order value of $50, that's 200 orders and roughly $350 in processing — total around **$389/mo**.

On **Wix Business Unlimited**, you pay $25/mo plus the same 2.9% + $0.30 Wix Payments rate. Those same 200 orders cost $350 in processing. Total: roughly **$375/mo**.

The gap nearly disappears. But here's what many sellers miss — the average Shopify store spends $50–$150/mo on premium apps (Source: Shopify community surveys, 2025). That tilts total cost higher for Shopify. You do get significantly more selling power for that spend, but the number is real.

**Hidden costs to watch:** Premium Shopify themes run $100–$400 one-time. Both platforms charge extra for advanced email sends beyond their free tiers. Shopify charges $79/mo for additional staff accounts on Basic. Wix includes limited collaborators.

## Ease of Use: Setup and Day-to-Day Store Management

Wix uses a true drag-and-drop editor. Move any element anywhere. Sellers who have never built a site can typically launch a basic Wix store in **1–2 hours**. Wix ADI — a tool that auto-builds a site from your answers to a few questions — can cut that down further.

Shopify uses a structured theme editor. You customize sections within a fixed layout, not free-drag elements across the page. Expect **2–4 hours** to launch a polished store, especially when configuring shipping zones (found under **Settings > Shipping and delivery**), tax rules, and product variants. The learning curve is steeper. But that structure stops design mistakes that quietly hurt conversions.

Shopify Magic AI tools — introduced in 2023 and expanded through 2026 — let you auto-generate product descriptions, edit product images, and write email copy directly inside the admin (Source: Shopify, 2026). If you manage hundreds of SKUs, this saves real hours.

**Verdict:** Wix is easier out of the box. Shopify takes more setup but pays off once you grow past a few dozen products. If you're ready to start, follow our [how to start a Shopify store](/how-to-start-a-shopify-store) walkthrough.

## Ecommerce Features: Selling Power Compared

This is where the gap between Shopify and Wix gets hard to ignore.

### Product Management

Shopify gives you **unlimited products on every paid plan**, with up to 3 option types — size, color, material, for example — and 100 variants per product. Apps from the Shopify App Store extend this further. Wix product limits vary by plan, and variant handling is more restricted. That's a real problem if you sell items across multiple sizes, colors, and materials.

### Abandoned Cart Recovery

Shopify includes abandoned cart emails on **all paid plans**, including Basic. Configure them under **Settings > Checkout** in the admin. Wix locks this feature behind Business Unlimited ($25/mo) and above.

The Baymard Institute (2025) puts the average cart abandonment rate at 70.19%. Recovery emails typically win back 5–14% of those lost sales. Having this on your cheapest plan matters.

### Subscriptions, Digital Products, and Dropshipping

Both platforms handle subscriptions, digital product delivery, and dropshipping. But Shopify goes deeper. Apps like Recharge for subscriptions and DSers for AliExpress dropshipping are tightly built for Shopify — more granular controls, more flexibility. Wix covers the basics. Complex subscription logic like build-a-box or prepaid plans? Wix falls short.

### International Selling

**Shopify Markets** handles multi-currency pricing, localized checkout, and duties and tax calculations for international buyers — all from one store, managed under **Settings > Markets**. **Wix Multilingual** translates content well, but the cross-border tools are weaker. Wix has no built-in duty estimation, which creates surprise costs that push international buyers to abandon their carts.

### B2B and Wholesale

Shopify Plus is the clear choice for B2B — wholesale channels, custom pricing rules, net payment terms. Wix has basic wholesale options but nothing close to enterprise level. For enterprise comparisons, see our [Shopify vs BigCommerce](/shopify-vs-bigcommerce) article.

**Real-world example:** Brooklyn candle brand *Homestead & Co.* launched on Wix in 2024 with 15 SKUs. After growing to 50 SKUs and adding international shipping, they moved to Shopify in early 2025. They reported a **40% revenue increase within six months** — driven mainly by Shopify Markets multi-currency checkout and abandoned cart recovery on the Basic plan.

## Payment Options and Checkout Experience

**Shopify Payments** is built into every Shopify store, powered by Stripe. It supports major credit cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and **Shop Pay** — Shopify's accelerated checkout that stores payment and shipping info for one-tap buying. According to Shopify (2025), Shop Pay lifts checkout conversion by up to 18% compared to standard checkouts. Shopify also supports **100+ third-party gateways**, including standalone Stripe and PayPal.

**Wix Payments** works in the US and accepts major cards, Apple Pay, and Google Pay. Wix supports far fewer third-party gateways. If your customers prefer regional or alternative payment methods, this is a real limitation. The Wix checkout is clean and functional — but customization options are minimal on standard plans.

Checkout customization separates the two further. **Shopify Plus** merchants can edit the checkout experience through extensibility APIs — adding custom fields, upsells, or loyalty redemption directly on the page. Standard Shopify plans offer limited tweaks. Wix gives almost no checkout customization at any tier. For brands focused on reducing friction at the final step, that matters.

## SEO and Marketing Tools: Driving US Traffic in 2026

### SEO Capabilities

Shopify auto-generates XML sitemaps, handles canonical tags, and produces clean URLs — for example, `/products/product-name`. The built-in blog is basic but functional for content marketing. See our [ecommerce SEO guide](/ecommerce-seo-guide) for deeper strategy.

Wix SEO Wiz and the Wix SEO Hub walk beginners through setup in a way that's genuinely useful — meta descriptions, alt text, the basics. Wix has improved URL flexibility since 2024. But product URLs still carry prefixes like `/product-page/product-name` that you can't fully remove. Shopify's flatter URL structure stays cleaner for ecommerce SEO and tends to perform better in Google Shopping results.

### Email Marketing

**Shopify Email** gives you 10,000 free emails per month. Extra sends cost $1 per 1,000 (Source: Shopify, 2026). Templates pull from your product catalog automatically. **Wix Email Marketing** includes send limits that vary by plan.

Both have drag-and-drop builders and basic automation. But Shopify's native Klaviyo integration — the most widely used email and SMS platform for ecommerce, available through the App Store — gives you segmentation, predictive analytics, and advanced flow building that neither platform's built-in tools can match.

### Social Selling and Google Shopping

Both platforms connect to **TikTok Shop, Instagram Shopping, and Facebook Shops** in 2026. Both link to **Google Merchant Center** for Shopping listings. Shopify's Google & YouTube channel app manages product feeds and Performance Max campaigns natively. Sellers running multiple channels often find Shopify's centralized order management cuts fulfillment errors.

## Apps and Integrations: Extending Your Store

The **Shopify App Store** has over 8,000 apps as of 2026 (Source: Shopify, 2026). Loyalty programs, advanced analytics, upsell tools — it's all there. The **Wix App Market** offers around 300+ ecommerce-relevant apps — far fewer, but Wix builds more into its core platform, so small stores need fewer add-ons.

Apps available only on Shopify — or with much deeper Shopify integration — include **Klaviyo** for email and SMS, **Judge.me** and **Loox** for photo and video reviews, and **Recharge** for subscriptions. Browse our [best Shopify apps in 2026](/best-shopify-apps-2026) list for specific picks.

The trade-off is real. The average Shopify merchant spends **$50–$150/mo on apps** (Source: Shopify community surveys, 2025). On Wix, you might spend $0–$30/mo because reviews and basic email come built in. This is one of the biggest factors in total cost of ownership — and one most merchants underestimate.

## Design and Templates: Making Your Store Look Pro

Shopify offers **100+ themes** with 13 free options in 2026. Premium themes cost **$100–$400 one-time** and are built for ecommerce conversions — optimized product grids, quick-view pop-ups, sticky add-to-cart buttons. Shopify limits layout freedom on purpose. That structure keeps page speeds fast and mobile experiences consistent. Google uses Core Web Vitals as a ranking signal, so this matters.

Wix has **800+ templates** with around 100 ecommerce-focused designs, and they're **all free**. Move anything anywhere. Total creative control. The downside: that freedom makes it easy to build layouts that hurt mobile performance or confuse buyers. Baymard Institute (2024) found that 67% of users leave a mobile site that's hard to navigate. Design freedom cuts both ways.

Both platforms deliver solid mobile responsiveness in 2026. If design flexibility is your priority, Wix wins. If conversion-optimized structure matters more, Shopify wins.

## Customer Support and Reliability

Shopify offers **24/7 support via live chat, phone, and email**, plus a large community forum and the Shopify Help Center. Plus merchants get dedicated support managers. Shopify guarantees **99.99% uptime** under its SLA (Source: Shopify, 2026).

Wix offers **24/7 callback support** and a solid help center, but live chat is limited on lower plans. Uptime is comparable in practice — though Wix doesn't publish an explicit SLA with the same transparency. For stores running flash sales where every hour of downtime is lost revenue, Shopify's reliability and direct support give more confidence.

## Who Should Choose Shopify vs Wix in 2026?

### Choose Shopify if:

- You plan to scale past **$5K/month in revenue**
- You need advanced inventory management with multiple variants
- You want multichannel selling across TikTok, Instagram, Amazon, and in-person via **Shopify POS**
- You're building a direct-to-consumer brand with growth ambitions
- You need international selling tools through **Shopify Markets**

### Choose Wix if:

- You're a freelancer, local business, or hobbyist adding a small shop to an existing site
- Your catalog is under 50 products with simple options
- Budget is your top priority and you want to stay under $25/mo
- You want a good general-purpose website that also sells a few items
- You value total design freedom over ecommerce-specific features

### The Gray Area

Wix is genuinely viable for stores doing **under $5K/month** with simple catalogs. Merchants selling 10–20 straightforward products with no complex automations can save real money on Wix without hitting walls. See our [Wix eCommerce review](/wix-ecommerce-review) for a closer look.

**Migration warning:** Moving from Wix to Shopify later is possible — export product CSVs and reimport them — but you'll rebuild pages, reinstall apps, and redo SEO settings from scratch. Wix doesn't support 301 redirect mapping to a new domain structure, so you'll likely lose some search rankings temporarily. Budget 2–4 weeks for a full migration, or use a tool like Cart2Cart to automate product transfers. Choose carefully upfront. If you're also weighing open-source options, see our [Shopify vs WooCommerce](/shopify-vs-woocommerce) comparison.

## FAQ

### Is Shopify better than Wix for ecommerce in 2026?

For most dedicated online stores, yes. Shopify has stronger sales tools, more payment options, and better scalability. Wix is the better choice if you want a full website with a small shop attached.

### Can Wix handle a high-volume store?

Wix handles moderate volume fine. But stores doing over $10K/month typically hit limits on variants, apps, and reporting. Shopify handles high volume more smoothly — especially with multi-location inventory and advanced analytics on higher-tier plans.

### Does Shopify charge transaction fees?

Shopify charges 2% on Basic, 1% on the Shopify plan, and 0.5% on Advanced if you don't use Shopify Payments. Use Shopify Payments and those platform fees disappear — credit card processing fees still apply.

### Which platform has better SEO — Shopify or Wix?

Shopify generally performs better for ecommerce SEO due to cleaner URL structures and faster page speeds. Wix has improved significantly since 2024 and works well for local or small-catalog stores — but its URL prefix structure remains a minor disadvantage.

### Can I migrate from Wix to Shopify later?

Yes, but it takes work. Export product CSVs from Wix and import them to Shopify, but you'll need to rebuild pages, apps, and SEO settings manually — or use a migration tool like Cart2Cart. Budget 2–4 weeks for a complete transition.

### Which is cheaper, Shopify or Wix?

Wix starts cheaper — $17/mo vs Shopify's $39/mo. Once you add apps and factor in Shopify's stronger sales tools, the gap narrows for serious sellers. But Wix stays the more affordable option for small stores with simple needs.

### Does Wix have abandoned cart recovery?

Yes, but only on Business Unlimited ($25/mo) and Business VIP ($35/mo). Shopify includes abandoned cart recovery on all paid plans, including Basic ($39/mo).
